# Full lesson standard

Every theory lesson is written in English for a beginner and must contain:

1. learning goals;
2. definitions of every new term before it is used;
3. a detailed concept explanation in small steps;
4. syntax or a minimal code pattern;
5. at least one runnable example;
6. a line-by-line walkthrough;
7. expected output and an explanation of the result;
8. an engineering or mathematics example where appropriate;
9. common mistakes and their corrections;
10. a safety or limitations note where calculations or AI are involved;
11. a “Try it yourself” exercise;
12. three or four understanding questions;
13. Previous, module index, and Next navigation;
14. a “Further reading” section linking to the exact official documentation used.

Python language and standard-library facts use Python 3.14 documentation as the primary technical reference. Third-party packages use their official documentation. Course prose and engineering examples must be original rather than copied from a reference.
